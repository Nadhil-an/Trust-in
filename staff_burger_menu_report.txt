--- Staff Member Burger Menu Features ---

Below is the complete list of features accessible from the top-left burger menu (SideDrawer) for a Staff Member:

PRIMARY MENU:
1. Home
2. My Donation
3. Report a Problem
4. Events
5. My Profile

STAFF-SPECIFIC FEATURES:
6. Upload Events (This appears only for Staff and Manager roles)

SECONDARY MENU:
7. About Us
8. Contact Us
9. Settings

ACCOUNT ACTIONS:
10. Logout

Note: The side drawer also displays the organization branding ("SREELAKSHMI CHARITABLE TRUST") at the top and a footer message ("Together for a Better Tomorrow") at the bottom.
