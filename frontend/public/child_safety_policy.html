Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Sree Lakshmi Charitable Trust | Kerala Charity Community (KCC)

Last updated: November 2026

Zero Tolerance Policy: Kerala Charity Community (KCC) maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), and any form of child endangerment or grooming.

1. Scope and Principles

This Child Safety Standards Policy applies to all users, members, staff, and contributors of the Kerala Charity Community (KCC) mobile application and related services operated by Sree Lakshmi Charitable Trust. Our primary mission is charitable welfare and community relief, and we are strictly committed to maintaining a safe, protected environment free from any child sexual exploitation or abuse.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following activities are strictly prohibited across all community features, posts, comments, profile information, and image uploads:

  • Producing, uploading, sharing, linking to, or soliciting Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Any form of Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE), including grooming, sexual extortion, or inappropriate communication directed at minors.
  • Depicting, encouraging, or facilitating sexual abuse, exploitation, or physical endangerment of children.
  • Sharing personal identifiable information of children without authorized guardian consent for verified charitable relief purposes.

3. In-App Reporting Mechanism

Kerala Charity Community empowers every member to report any content, post, or user account that raises safety or child welfare concerns:

  • Report Feature: Every public post and community update has a dedicated reporting option (accessible via the post options menu). Users can select "Child Safety / Inappropriate Content" to immediately flag the content.
  • Priority Review: Reports involving potential child safety violations are treated as critical high-priority alerts and reviewed immediately by trust moderators.
  • Direct Email Reporting: Users may also report urgent child safety concerns directly to our dedicated safety team at sreelakshmichitfins@gmail.com.

4. Content Moderation and Account Enforcement

Upon detection or receipt of a report alleging CSAE or child endangerment:

  • Immediate Removal: The reported content, media, or comment is immediately removed from public view pending investigation.
  • Account Termination: Any user found attempting to distribute CSAM or engage in child sexual exploitation will face immediate, permanent account ban and revocation of trust membership.
  • Preservation of Evidence: Offending data is securely preserved solely for the purpose of law enforcement reporting.

5. Mandatory Reporting to Authorities

We comply fully with regional, national, and international laws regarding child protection:

  • We report any confirmed instance of CSAM or CSAE to regional law enforcement authorities and cybercrime cells in India (Cyber Crime Cell, Ministry of Home Affairs).
  • We collaborate with international safety agencies, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, to eradicate child exploitation material from digital platforms.
