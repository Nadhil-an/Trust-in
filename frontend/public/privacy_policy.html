Privacy Policy

Sree Lakshmi Charitable Trust | Kerala Charity Community (KCC) Mobile Application

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Sree Lakshmi Charitable Trust ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data provided by members, donors, field executives, and community participants using the Kerala Charity Community (KCC) mobile application and related web dashboard services.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or participate as a member, donor, or authorized officer in the app, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Identity & Contact Info: Full name, phone number, email address, profile photo, and registered residential address.
  • Donation & Membership Data: Membership ID, referral code, donation receipts, contribution history, and incentive transactions.
  • Financial & Payment Verification: Transaction reference IDs, payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer, UPI reference) used exclusively for verifiable accounting and statutory audit receipts. We do not store credit/debit card PINs or net-banking passwords.
  • Device Permissions:
    • Camera & Media Storage: To allow users and field staff to take and upload receipts, document scans, or profile images.
    • Location (Coarse/Fine): To verify field staff visit locations during charity relief, survey collection, and counter reconciliations.
    • Push Notifications: To notify you of donation acknowledgments, bonus credits, and trust announcements.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly to fulfill the charitable, administrative, and statutory objectives of the Trust, including:

  • Issuing official donation receipts and verifying charitable contributions.
  • Managing member directory, referral networks, and staff incentive accounts.
  • Providing customer support, answering community inquiries, and delivering administrative notifications.
  • Preventing fraudulent collections and complying with Indian Trust regulatory reporting standards.

3. Data Protection and Encryption

We implement stringent technical security controls to safeguard your data:

  • Sensitive personal details (such as phone numbers, government IDs, and account numbers) are encrypted in our database using industry-standard AES encryption.
  • All network communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS (SSL/TLS).
  • Access to management records is strictly limited to authorized trust personnel with role-based access control.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We respect your privacy. Sree Lakshmi Charitable Trust never sells, rents, or commercializes your personal information to third-party marketers or advertisers. Information is only shared when:

  • Required by law, court order, or official governmental statutory audits.
  • Necessary to process transactions through verified banking channels and payment gateway partners.

5. Account Deletion and Data Retention

Users have the right to request access to their data or request account deletion. To request deletion of your account and personal records, you may email us at support@sreelakshmicharity.org or contact the trust administration office. Upon verification, your account profile will be deactivated, retaining only those transactional records required by law for charitable auditing purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app and services are directed towards adults (18 years of age and older). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
